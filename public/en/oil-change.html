Oil change in Tallinn

We change engine oil and the oil filter at our workshop in the Kadaka area, Iva 12. An oil change costs 85 € including the oil (up to 5 l) and the oil filter. By arrangement we also work evenings and weekends.

What is included

We drain the old oil, replace the oil filter and fill the engine with new oil. Up to 5 litres of oil is included. If your engine takes more, the extra oil is added to the price.

If you like, we can replace the cabin or air filter at the same time (10 € labour, filter extra).

How to book

Text us at +372 5698 4655 or use the contact form. Tell us the car make, model, year and engine (for example Volvo V40 1.8 petrol), and we will pick the right oil and filter and give you a free time.
