What is included

In a full change we take the tyres off the rims, fit your new or seasonal tyres, balance the wheels and put them back on the car. The price is for 4 tyres and depends on the rim size and on whether the rims are steel or alloy.

Run-flat tyres cost 5 € extra. Rims with tyre pressure sensors have a 40 € risk fee, because the sensors need extra care during the change. We are not responsible for old or corroded sensors breaking. We do not change tyres on 21–23 inch rims.

How to book

Text us at +372 5698 4655 or use the contact form. Tell us the car make and the rim size (for example 17"), and we will give you the price and a free time. The rim size is on the side of the tyre: 225/45 R17 means a 17-inch rim.
