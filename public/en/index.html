Our services in detail

Maintenance, oil change and tyre change

An oil change with oil and filter costs 85 €. A full tyre change (tyres off and on the rims, balancing, wheels on the car) costs from 35 € for 4 tyres, depending on the rim size and whether the rims are steel or alloy. Run-flat tyres cost 5 € extra and rims with pressure sensors have a 40 € risk fee. A filter change costs 10 € and a battery change 15 €, parts extra. Other maintenance is 35 € per hour.

Brakes and suspension

We work on brakes, suspension and steering, including brake pads and wheel bearings. Brake pad replacement costs 40 € per axle, pads and discs 60 €, parts extra. Other work 35 €/h.

Repairs and diagnostics

If a warning light comes on or the car behaves strangely, we start with diagnostics (30 €). We also do engine, electrical, clutch and gearbox work, plus battery, starter, alternator, belt and chain jobs.

Bodywork and cosmetics

We repair scratches and smaller paint and rust damage, polish cloudy headlights (40 € a pair) and tint windows (from 180 €). Bodywork prices depend on the damage, so describe it in your request. You can send a photo of the damage on Instagram.

Sale photos, car photos and car videos

Sale photos (60 €): clean, well-lit photos that make your listing stand out on auto24 or Facebook. Creative photos (from 100 €): stylish car photos for yourself or social media. Creative car video (300 €): an edited car video for Instagram or TikTok, examples at @jostvisuals. Before the shoot we can clean the car and polish the headlights.
